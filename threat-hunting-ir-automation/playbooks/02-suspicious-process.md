# Playbook 02 - Suspicious process
1. Capture image, command line, parent, user, timestamp, and endpoint.
2. Validate signer, path, hash, and deployment context.
3. Pivot to related file, network, and authentication events.
4. Distinguish routine administration from unexpected execution.
5. Record supported ATT&CK mapping and rationale.
Response: preserve evidence; do not kill processes or delete files automatically.
