# Playbook 03 - IOC match
1. Verify exact indicator type and value.
2. Check provenance, age, confidence, and freshness.
3. Identify process, user, asset owner, and business context.
4. Search adjacent telemetry.
5. Consider shared hosting, stale indicators, and benign test data.
6. Document confidence and next pivots.
Do not block an IP/domain based solely on a watchlist match.
