# Playbook 01 - Suspicious authentication
1. Validate host, account, source, event IDs, and time range.
2. Check with the account owner for expected activity or maintenance.
3. Look for a later successful logon and review its source/logon type.
4. Correlate identity and endpoint events.
5. Map to ATT&CK T1110 only when evidence supports the hypothesis.
6. Document evidence and disposition.
Response: use approved identity processes; do not disable accounts automatically.
